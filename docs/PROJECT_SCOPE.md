# CareerLaunch Studio Project Scope

Last updated: 2026-07-03

## Project Description

CareerLaunch Studio is a subscription SaaS that helps job seekers create resumes, CVs, and cover letters with guided structure, role-specific wording suggestions, quality checks, template customization, and export options.

## In Scope

- User accounts and secure resume storage.
- Resume builder with structured sections: contact, summary, work history, education, skills, certifications, projects, and optional custom sections.
- One original ATS-friendly resume template for Sprint 1, expanding to a small curated library in later sprints.
- Guided content suggestions and rewrite assistance.
- Resume quality scoring with actionable checks.
- PDF export in Sprint 1; DOCX and TXT later.
- Cover-letter builder after resume flow is stable.
- Stripe subscriptions with a free tier and paid export/features.
- Founder-mode analytics for activation, conversion, export, and churn.

## Non-Goals

- Copying Zety's brand, templates, protected copy, proprietary content, or exact user interface.
- Building a job board in the MVP.
- Realtime collaborative editing.
- Enterprise HR applicant tracking.
- Native mobile apps.
- Multi-language localization before product-market signal.
- Complex AI agents that automatically apply to jobs.

## Assumptions

- The first market is English-speaking job seekers in the United States.
- The product is web-first and self-serve.
- The business model is freemium plus subscription.
- The initial team is small, so operational simplicity matters.
- Users value speed, confidence, professional design, and export quality more than deep customization.

## Constraints

- Resume output must be readable by applicant tracking systems.
- Subscription flows must be clear and cancellation-friendly.
- User data includes sensitive career history and must be handled as private personal data.
- AI-generated content must be reviewable and editable by the user.

## Risks

- The resume-builder market is crowded and paid acquisition can be expensive.
- Trust is fragile if pricing, trial conversion, or cancellation feels opaque.
- Generated content can become generic without high-quality role context.
- PDF rendering can become a quality bottleneck if not treated as a core product feature.

## Success Definition

The MVP succeeds when a first-time user can create an account, build a polished resume from guided inputs, receive useful improvement suggestions, export a PDF, and understand the paid value without confusion.
